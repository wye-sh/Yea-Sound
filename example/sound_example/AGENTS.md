# sound_example

Sound's example view (component sound; never in a release): pads for every behaviour on the left (one-shots, loops, the engine's four cut-offs, music, 3D, and voices: two versions of one sound and a stranger whose values come from code), and on the right a listener (drag it) with three beacons circling and footsteps walking around it. With `sound` in feature Sound Example of view Sound. `Example.cpp`; sounds in `Sounds/`, made by `../../tools/sounds.py example Sounds`. Table `Demo` (the loops and toggles): Reset stops everything and turns every pad off.
